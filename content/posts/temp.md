I get asked a lot about my interests. I have a lot of interests. I'm somewhat intentional with how I spend my time practicing these interests. I'll dive into what I've done and how it all ties together under the umbrella of self-expression.

Figure skating was a hard sport. It's a sport, not an art.

Climbing is surprisingly expressive. I see it with a modicum of how mathematicians strive for an elegant solution. Everyone's objective on the wall is the same but how you arrive there is to your own liking.

Dance feels like a higher level abstraction to both figure skating and climbing in some ways. But contemporary dance speaks to me. I'm not sure why.

The world constantly finds ways to challenge us. It's important to keep feeling small in a world so big. And to keep asking yourself what you can bring to yourself and others.

Self-expression and problem-solving often go hand-in-hand with how I approach the world. I have ideas and to execute them requires planning. That's what keeps me motivated to keep learning. What keeps me inspired to keep exploring. It was figure skating, then climbing, and then dance. Tomorrow, it might be painting or double-dutch. And I expect my approach to be the same.
